HOW TO READ

What do these figures mean?

Population. Police-recorded road crashes with deaths or injuries. Property-damage-only crashes are excluded.

Severity. Severe classes are source codes 1, 2 and 3. Fatal-crash charts use only code 1 (“with deaths”). Neither is the number of people killed.

Map. Only records with usable coordinates appear on the map. Geolocation improved from 2022; compare years alongside coverage.

Interpretation. Road types, municipalities and cells show recorded counts. Without traffic volume or distance travelled, these are not user-level risks.
